Frequently Asked Questions

Website Development in Jaipur

Do you build websites for businesses in Jaipur?

Yes. I am a frontend web developer based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and I build websites for local shops, coaching institutes, clinics, startups and professionals. I also work with clients across India remotely, with calls and updates over WhatsApp.

What website services do you offer?

I offer business website development, frontend development, React.js development, UI/UX design, responsive design, website performance optimization, API integration, and bug fixing and support for existing websites.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the number of pages, the features you need and how much custom design is involved. Message me on WhatsApp with your requirements and I will share the details and pricing for your project.

How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the size of the website and how quickly content is shared. After I understand your requirements, I give you a clear estimate before starting.

Will my website work on mobile and be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every website is built mobile-first and responsive, with clean semantic HTML, fast loading and proper meta tags, which are the foundation for good search visibility. Nobody can honestly guarantee a Google ranking, but a technically solid site gives you the best start.
